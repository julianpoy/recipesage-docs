---
title: Importing From Mela
description: Import your Mela recipes into RecipeSage. Upload .melarecipes (or individual .melarecipe) files exported from Mela to transfer your collection.
---

You can import recipes exported by Mela by uploading them via the import page. RecipeSage accepts both Mela's bulk archive (`.melarecipes`) and an individual recipe file (`.melarecipe`).

[https://recipesage.com/app/settings/import/mela](https://recipesage.com/app/settings/import/mela)

To export your entire library from Mela on desktop:

1. Right-click the **All** section in the left side menu.
2. Click **All Recipes**.
3. Specify a location in which to save the `.melarecipes` archive.

You can also export a smaller subset by right-clicking any other category (Favorites, Want to Cook, or a custom category) and choosing its export option.
