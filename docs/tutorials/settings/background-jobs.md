The `Side Menu -> Settings -> View All Background Jobs` page lists every background job on your account, newest first.

## What Appears Here

Background jobs are long-running tasks that continue even if you close the app once started. This page shows all of them in one place, including:

- **Imports** - recipe data imports from other apps and formats.
- **Exports** - exports of your recipe data ([📖 Exporting Data](./export.md)).
- **Cookbook PDFs** - cookbooks created with the [📖 Cookbook Generator](../tools/cookbook-generator.md).

## Job Status

Each job shows when it was created and its current status:

- **Created, will begin soon.** - the job is queued.
- **Running...** - the job is in progress, with a progress bar.
- **Complete.** - the job finished successfully.
- **Failed.** - the job did not finish; the reason is shown where available.

## Downloading Results

Completed exports and cookbooks show a download icon on the right. Click the job to download its result. Imports do not produce a downloadable file.
