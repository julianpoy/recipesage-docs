A quick reference tool for the most common cooking temperatures. Open it from `Side Menu -> Tools -> Cooking Temperatures`.

While it's a pretty simple tool, it's handy to not have to "Google for it" when you need a quick temperature check while cooking.

## Chip legend

- **USDA safe min**: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service minimum internal temperature.
- **Preference**: a doneness preference or technique target - not a safety recommendation.
- **Oil temp**: deep-fry oil temperature, not the food's internal temperature.
- **Smoke point**: the temperature at which a cooking fat begins to smoke and break down. Treat it as a ceiling rather than a target.
- **Bacteria zone**: a range to *avoid*, not a target.
- **Rest N min**: USDA-recommended rest time after cooking.

## Notes

- Doneness rows below the USDA safe minimum are personal preference and may represent risk.
- Always use a food thermometer in the thickest part of the food, away from bone and fat.

Safe-minimum temperatures from the USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service and FoodSafety.gov.
