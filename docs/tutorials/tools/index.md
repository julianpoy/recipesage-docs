The `Side Menu -> Tools` page (titled **Tools & Helpers** in the app) is a collection of utilities for working with your recipes.

<img className="screenshot screenshot-wide" src="/img/tools.png" width="2844" height="2343" alt="Tools and Helpers page listing the available utilities"></img>

## Available Tools

- [📖 Cookbook Generator](./cookbook-generator.md) - Generate a printable PDF cookbook from the recipes in one of your labels.
- [📖 Nutrition Calculator](./nutrition-calculator.md) - Add up the nutrition across several recipes and scale it by servings.
- [📖 Search by Ingredients](./search-by-ingredients.md) - Find recipes you can cook from the ingredients you already have.
- [📖 Measurement Converter](./measurement-converter.md) - Convert between volumes, weights, ingredient amounts, and oven temperatures.
- [📖 Cooking Temperatures](./cooking-temperatures.md) - Safe internal temperatures, doneness levels, oven settings, and deep-fry oil targets.
- [📖 Pan & Bakeware Converter](./pan-bakeware-converter.md) - Swap pan sizes, scale ingredients by capacity, and adjust bake time and temperature.
- [📖 Cooking Assistant](../assistant/index.md) - Chat with an AI assistant about cooking and your recipes.
